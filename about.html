About

A resource kit,
maintained in public.

Community Tech Kit is a project of the Good Software Foundation, an independent non-profit that equips people from underserved and minority communities with technology skills and backs the tools their communities ask for.

Why this kit exists

Small non-profits lose hours to problems that have already been solved: a discount program they never heard of, books set up the hard way, the same bank statement re-typed every month. We collect those solutions in one place, in plain language, free for anyone to use.

How to contribute

Everything here lives in a public repository. Suggest a deal, fix a guide, or add a QuickBooks recipe by opening an issue or pull request, or simply email us. Content is licensed CC BY 4.0 - use it freely.
